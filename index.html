Financial Literacy & Fundamentals

3 Core Business Concepts Every Buyer Must Know

Why a 33% royalty cut sounds steep initially, but offers strategic financial advantages under specific circumstances:
01

CapEx vs. OpEx Tradeoff

CapEx (Capital Expenditure): Building an independent sushi shop requires $150,000 - $250,000 AUD upfront for exhaust hoods, grease traps, three-phase power, and coolrooms, plus costly make-good demolition liabilities upon lease termination.

OpEx (Operating Expenditure): Coles absorbs this $200k+ building cost and electricity bills entirely, in exchange for taking 33% of gross sales.

Strategic Takeaway: The 33% cut effectively trades variable operating revenue for zero fitout debt and zero make-good lease liabilities.

02

Buying a Job vs. True Business

Buying a Job: If net profit only covers the hours worked at standard market wage rates ($34-$36/hr), this is not an investment—it is paying $70,000 to secure self-employment.

True Business: A genuine commercial enterprise generates net surplus profit AFTER market wages are paid to operational staff or replacement labor.

Discussion Objective: What is the effective hourly return on our operational time? Does it surpass employment earnings?

03

The 1-Week-Old Greenfield Risk

Zero Goodwill: Trading for only 7 days means there are no loyal repeat customers and no historical quarterly BAS tax filings to verify.

Grand Opening Honeymoon Effect: Initial foot traffic often reflects temporary curiosity, which can taper off by 15-25% once footfall normalizes.

Critical Investigation: Why is the seller divesting after only 1 week? Physical exhaustion or a professional turnkey developer?

Strategic Deal Due Diligence

5 Core Due Diligence Q&As for Son & Investors

Master these questions and strategic evaluations to protect our investment:
Q1
Why is the seller exiting after trading for only 1 week?

Strategic Analysis: A business does not normally sell in week one unless it is a pre-arranged turnkey development or the operator faced an overwhelming operational shock. We must determine whether the problem is footfall structure or operator stamina.

🟢 Green Flag (Acceptable): The seller is a serial franchise builder whose business model is taking initial handover risks, or an investor with zero kitchen experience who realized they dislike 5:30 AM prep.
🔴 Red Flag (Walk Away): The seller realizes Coles customer traffic consists only of raw grocery shoppers who do not buy ready-to-eat sushi, rendering break-even impossible.
Q2
What is the remaining tenure of the Master Agreement between Coles and Snowfox?

Strategic Analysis: You are a sub-licensee inside Coles. Even if your franchise agreement is for 5 years, if Coles' head contract with Snowfox expires in 6 months or Coles holds unilateral redevelopment rights, your tenure is in jeopardy.

🟢 Green Flag: Coles and franchisor hold an active multi-year national contract with at least 3-5 years guaranteed tenure and clear relocation compensation provisions.
🔴 Red Flag: Supermarket is scheduled for a major remodel within 12 months, or Coles reserves the right to terminate kiosk operators with only 30 days notice.
Q3
How can we reduce the daily 20-box food waste (~11% loss rate)?

Strategic Analysis: Discarding 20 boxes daily at $2 raw cost equals ~$280/wk down the drain (and lost retail sales of ~$1,500/wk). We need to know if the franchisor permits late-afternoon dynamic markdowns (e.g., $10 boxes after 4:30 PM).

🟢 Green Flag: POS system allows end-of-day markdown stickers, converting surplus rolls into pure cash flow rather than dumpster waste.
🔴 Red Flag: Franchisor strictly prohibits discounting to protect brand image, forcing full disposal and eroding owner profit.
Q4
Can we negotiate a 2 to 4-week "Trial Period" before final settlement?

Strategic Analysis: Because the store has zero BAS tax history, paying 100% upfront on day 7 is excessive risk. The standard protective tactic is placing a 10% holding deposit in a solicitor's trust account while family members work inside for a 2-4 week trial.

🟢 Green Flag: Seller welcomes buyers into the kitchen for hands-on trial and training, guaranteeing average sales verification before full fund release.
🔴 Red Flag: Seller rushes an immediate unconditional contract and refuses to let buyers observe real daily register transactions.
Q5
What is the exact daily physical schedule required for two operators?

Strategic Analysis: Supermarket sushi requires fresh morning rollout. Knowing whether rice cookers and sheeters can be prepped in 1.5 hours vs. 3.5 hours dictates whether operators work a sustainable 7-8 hour day or an exhausting 12-hour grind.

🟢 Green Flag: Automatic Rice Sheeter and Roll Cutter allow 180 boxes to be completed by 10:30 AM with steady afternoon maintenance.
🔴 Red Flag: Heavy manual rolling required due to faulty machines, requiring 5:00 AM to 6:30 PM presence without breaks.
